About Me

The Origin

I like systems that follow logic without excuses, and that need for control is what pulled me into AI. At first, I did not even know it was all driven by math. Discovering that AI actually works through mathematical predictions made decoding its core logic way more exciting, since math was already my strong suit.

The Philosophy

I do not trust black-box tools that hide how a system actually works under the hood. Instead of taking shortcuts with pre-built libraries, I build algorithms, pure NumPy logic, and mathematical engines entirely from scratch. For me, real engineering means commanding every single layer of how a machine calculates, learns, and processes data.

The Vision

I focus on engineering custom AI architectures, high-speed data pipelines, and logic-driven engines built for raw performance. Rather than just wrapping existing APIs, I build scalable, self-reliant systems where I direct the entire execution flow, from raw data ingestion to the final real-time predictions.
