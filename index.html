CORE PRINCIPLE

"True engineering means understanding every layer of how a machine stores, calculates, and learns from data."

PHILOSOPHY & VISION

First-Principles Systems Engineering

My work is guided by a simple belief: to build truly reliable and intelligent systems, you must understand the mathematics and hardware mechanics underneath, and automate the repetitive work that slows human thinking down.

THE APPROACH

Understanding From Scratch

Instead of treating machine learning as a black box, I prefer building core algorithms, matrices, and tensors from the ground up. Writing mathematical foundations from scratch gives me complete clarity over how data moves in memory, how models converge, and where real performance bottlenecks hide.

THE FOUNDATION

Automating the Data Bottleneck

Most of data science is still slowed down by manual data cleaning, while automated training tools often assume raw data is already clean. I built NullHunter to bridge this gap, an autonomous data refinery that prepares large, messy datasets on standard hardware so engineers can focus on building intelligence rather than fixing tables.

THE VISION

Accessible Autonomous Intelligence

Data refining is the first step toward a larger goal. Through OmniCore Systems and OmniCore Tabular Intelligence (OTI), I am working toward unified, self-reliant systems that automate the complex layers of AI, making it practical for anyone to build and train high-quality models without massive infrastructure.
